The Eastover Rodent Situation

Eastover spans the eastern part of Cumberland County between US-301, I-95, and the Bladen County line. The community is mostly rural-residential with older single-family homes on sandy coastal-plain soil, interspersed with working farmland and pine plantation. The housing stock varies in age and condition, but the dominant pattern is older construction with the foundation-level conditions characteristic of mid-20th-century rural housing.

📋 Real Scenario

A homeowner on agricultural-adjacent acreage called us after finding rat droppings in the garage. The garage shared a wall with an attached barn that had become a rat refuge. We treated both structures together, sealed 22 exclusion points along the shared wall and barn perimeter, and recommended a feed-storage protocol change. Six months later the property remained clear.

The I-95 and US-301 commercial nodes at the western edge of the community add a layered pressure factor that purely-residential rural areas don't have: truck stops, fuel depots, and distribution facilities along the interstate corridor sustain commercial-adjacent Norway rat populations. Properties within several hundred feet of those nodes see slightly elevated pressure compared to properties deeper into the residential interior.
