Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Raeford

UC IPM notes that Norway rats dig burrows along foundations and beneath rubbish and woodpiles, while roof rats nest high in attics and wall voids. For a home in Raeford, both get checked: the ground along the foundation and the roofline.

NC State advises screening attic and crawl space vents with quarter-inch hardware cloth and says expanding foam alone will not hold, because rats and mice chew through it. For Raeford homes that means metal and mesh at each opening.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Raeford

  • Foundation edges: look for burrows beside the wall and under steps, sheds and woodpiles, which are Norway rat signs.
  • Attic: look for droppings, rub marks and shredded insulation. UC IPM says roof rats nest above ground in attics and wall voids.
  • Sheds, fences and outbuildings: each is a separate route in and gets the same sealing as the house.
  • Pipe, wire and dryer vent entries: UC IPM says gaps wider than a quarter inch need metal or concrete, with steel wool only as a temporary plug.
  • Kitchen and pantry: store food in sealed containers. NC State puts a mouse at 50 to 75 droppings a day, so droppings in a cabinet add up fast.
