Erwin and Its Rodent Challenges

Erwin developed as a textile mill town on the Cape Fear River and retains a significant inventory of pre-WWII and mid-century housing. Older residential structures accumulate entry-point gaps: at sill plates, utility penetrations, eave vents, and foundation offsets, that give both Norway rats and house mice ready access. The Cape Fear River at Erwin creates the same burrow-pressure dynamic seen in Lillington: rats follow the river corridor and drainage infrastructure into the older residential core.
