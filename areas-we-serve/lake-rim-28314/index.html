Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Lake Rim

NC State advises screening attic and crawl space vents with quarter-inch hardware cloth and says expanding foam alone will not hold, because rats and mice chew through it. For Lake Rim homes that means metal and mesh at each opening.

UC IPM notes that Norway rats dig burrows along foundations and beneath rubbish and woodpiles, while roof rats nest high in attics and wall voids. For a home in Lake Rim, both get checked: the ground along the foundation and the roofline.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Lake Rim

  • Attic: look for droppings, rub marks and shredded insulation. UC IPM says roof rats nest above ground in attics and wall voids.
  • Sheds, fences and outbuildings: each is a separate route in and gets the same sealing as the house.
  • Pipe, wire and dryer vent entries: UC IPM says gaps wider than a quarter inch need metal or concrete, with steel wool only as a temporary plug.
  • Kitchen and pantry: store food in sealed containers. NC State puts a mouse at 50 to 75 droppings a day, so droppings in a cabinet add up fast.
  • Garage and storage: keep boxes off the floor and away from walls so droppings and nests are easy to spot.
