Frequently Asked. Lake Rim Rodent Control

Why do I keep getting rats even after treatment near Lake Rim Park?

The park's wetland and pond areas sustain a consistent outdoor Norway rat population. Treating your property reduces interior activity, but the outdoor source remains. Structural exclusion, sealing all entry points, is the only approach that provides lasting results when a large outdoor reservoir is adjacent.

What type of rodents are most common in Lake Rim?

Norway rats are the dominant call driver, particularly for properties near the park. House mice are also present throughout 28314 in homes with garage seal and utility penetration gaps. Roof rat activity is less common than in canopy-heavy neighborhoods like Haymount.

Does living near Lake Rim mean I have more rodent risk?

Slightly elevated, yes. Waterway proximity correlates with Norway rat pressure because rats use water-edge and drainage corridors as travel routes. The risk is manageable with proper foundation-perimeter exclusion and routine inspection.

What does drainage-corridor treatment involve?

Mapping the drainage routes near the property, placing bait stations at points where drainage lines approach the structure, and sealing any foundation-level entry that drainage corridors might lead to. This is a targeted Norway rat protocol rather than generic exclusion.
