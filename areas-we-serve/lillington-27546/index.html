Understanding Rodent Pressure in Lillington

Lillington sits on the Cape Fear River in central Harnett County, about 30 miles north of Fayetteville. The river corridor is the primary Norway rat pressure driver: rats follow riverbanks, drainage channels, and utility right-of-ways from water sources into the older downtown core and surrounding neighborhoods.
