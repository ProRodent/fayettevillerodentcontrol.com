What part of 28303 sees the most rodent activity?

Blocks closest to Cross Creek and its stormwater tributaries see the highest Norway rat pressure. Older residential blocks with mature tree canopy see more roof rat activity. Commercial-adjacent blocks have sustained perimeter pressure from dumpster area foraging.

Do you cover the whole 28303 ZIP?

How do I know which service I need?

Call (844) 635-0403 and describe what you're observing, scratching location (overhead vs. crawl space), dropping location, or visual sighting details. We triage over the phone and confirm species ID at inspection.

Why do well-maintained homes still develop rodent issues?

Cosmetic maintenance doesn't address foundation-level conditions. A fresh exterior paint job doesn't fix a corroded crawl-space vent screen or a deteriorated mortar joint at the foundation base, those are the actual entry points for rodents.

Are there discounts for inspection without confirmed activity?

We treat preventive inspection the same as active-issue inspection: same scope, same documentation, same flat inspection fee. Confirmed-activity treatments include the inspection cost in the treatment quote.
