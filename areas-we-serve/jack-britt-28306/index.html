FAQ. Rodent Treatment in Jack Britt

Do I need exclusion sealing if I live near a detention pond in Jack Britt?

Yes, bait programs reduce the perimeter population but can't eliminate an outdoor Norway rat reservoir. Structural exclusion sealing is what prevents the perimeter population from entering your specific structure, and it's the only approach that provides lasting results when a large outdoor source is adjacent.

My house is newer but I have mice. How did they get in?

Newer construction has garage door seal wear, door sweep gaps, and utility penetrations that are commonly unsealed in production homes. House mice need only a quarter-inch gap. Our mouse proofing survey covers the primary mouse entry categories in Jack Britt-era slab construction.

Are newer Jack Britt subdivisions really at lower rodent risk than older neighborhoods?
