How Cameron's Environment Shapes Rodent Activity

Cameron is best known for its antique district along US-1, with a collection of historic buildings, warehouses, and commercial structures that are rodent-pressure challenges in their own right. Old commercial buildings, with their void spaces, multiple-decade accumulation of structural gaps, and often inconsistent maintenance histories, provide extensive rodent harborage that's difficult to eliminate without systematic exclusion work.
