Frequently Asked. Arran Lakes Rodent Control

Why do I have mice in a house that's only a few years old?

New construction doesn't mean rodent-tight construction. Plumbing, HVAC, and electrical penetrations in production homes frequently have gaps at exterior walls that were never sealed. Garage door bottom seals also wear within a few years. House mice need only a quarter-inch gap to enter.

Are the retention ponds near Arran Lakes a rodent issue?

Yes. Earthen pond banks provide ideal Norway rat burrowing substrate and consistent water access. Properties adjacent to the ponds see higher sustained perimeter pressure than those further into the subdivision interior.

Are slab-on-grade homes in Arran Lakes harder to treat than older crawl-space construction?

Different, not harder. Slab homes don't have the foundation-level burrow exposure but do have utility-penetration gaps where pipes and conduits enter through the slab. Treatment focuses on those specific entry points rather than crawl space sealing.
