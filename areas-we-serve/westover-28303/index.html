Is Westover at risk for both types of rats?

Potentially both, depending on location within the neighborhood. Norway rats are present throughout Westover via drainage corridors. Roof rat exposure depends on the specific block's tree canopy. Our inspection surveys both zones.

Does Cross Creek Park create rodent problems for nearby houses?

Yes. Greenway and park areas sustain Norway rat populations year-round through vegetation, drainage, and limited disturbance. Properties within a few blocks see sustained perimeter pressure that structural exclusion addresses better than ongoing bait programs.

Why does mid-century construction era matter for rodent treatment?
