Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Southern Pines

NC State advises screening attic and crawl space vents with quarter-inch hardware cloth and says expanding foam alone will not hold, because rats and mice chew through it. For Southern Pines homes that means metal and mesh at each opening.

UC IPM notes that Norway rats dig burrows along foundations and beneath rubbish and woodpiles, while roof rats nest high in attics and wall voids. For a home in Southern Pines, both get checked: the ground along the foundation and the roofline.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Southern Pines

  • Rooflines and trees: UC IPM lists trimming limbs back from the roof as a way to cut the route roof rats use.
  • Trash and pet food: keep both in sealed containers off the ground, since food outside keeps a rat population close to the house.
  • Door bottoms and garage door seals: NC State advises keeping the clearance at the floor to a quarter inch or less, with kick plates or rodent-proof sweeps where doors take wear.
  • Foundation edges: look for burrows beside the wall and under steps, sheds and woodpiles, which are Norway rat signs.
  • Attic: look for droppings, rub marks and shredded insulation. UC IPM says roof rats nest above ground in attics and wall voids.
