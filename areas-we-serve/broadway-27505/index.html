How Broadway's Environment Shapes Rodent Activity

Broadway occupies the northwestern corner of Harnett County near the Lee County line, with a rural agricultural character. The community sits at the edge of the Sandhills, with sandy soils transitioning toward the NC piedmont but still loose enough to facilitate Norway rat burrowing at foundation perimeters. Surrounding farmland maintains baseline rodent populations year-round.

What are the signs of Norway rats in Broadway NC homes?

Ground-level burrow holes (2-3 inches wide) near foundations or under decks, grease smears along foundation walls, capsule-shaped droppings at crawl space vents, and scratching sounds from below-grade areas.
