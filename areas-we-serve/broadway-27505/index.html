Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Broadway

NC State says gaps of about a quarter inch let mice in and a half inch lets adult rats in. At a property in Broadway, the inspection covers the garage and exterior door clearance, pipe and wire entries through walls and the crawl space and attic vents.

UC IPM notes that Norway rats dig burrows along foundations and beneath rubbish and woodpiles, while roof rats nest high in attics and wall voids. For a home in Broadway, both get checked: the ground along the foundation and the roofline.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Broadway

  • Pipe, wire and dryer vent entries: UC IPM says gaps wider than a quarter inch need metal or concrete, with steel wool only as a temporary plug.
  • Kitchen and pantry: store food in sealed containers. NC State puts a mouse at 50 to 75 droppings a day, so droppings in a cabinet add up fast.
  • Garage and storage: keep boxes off the floor and away from walls so droppings and nests are easy to spot.
  • Attic and crawl space vents: screen them with quarter-inch hardware cloth. Foam alone and chicken wire do not hold.
  • Rooflines and trees: UC IPM lists trimming limbs back from the roof as a way to cut the route roof rats use.
