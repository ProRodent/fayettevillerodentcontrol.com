Why am I getting rodents in a new construction home in Angier?

Site clearing during development displaces established populations. Rodents from surrounding agricultural land also migrate toward new residential areas. New builds can have unsealed utility penetrations and crawl space gaps at the time of occupancy.

Is mouse proofing available in Angier?

Yes. We identify entry points and seal them with hardware cloth, expanding foam, and sheet metal as appropriate. Mouse proofing is often a component of a full removal program.

How much does rodent exclusion cost in Angier?

Exclusion sealing runs $400-$900 for a typical residential scope. Full programs with removal and exclusion run $800-$1,800. Call for a same-day quote.

Why do new homes in Angier have rodent issues at all?

Construction displacement. When farmland is cleared for a new subdivision, the field rat population doesn't disappear. It relocates to whatever cover remains, including the newly-built homes. New construction quality varies, and inadequate sealing during build accelerates the problem.

How long does post-construction rodent pressure last in Angier subdivisions?

Usually 2-4 years from initial development. After the displaced population either disperses or is addressed through treatment, pressure typically drops to normal suburban levels. Properly sealed homes don't see significant impact even during the peak displacement period.
