What Massey Hill Residents Should Know About Rodents

FAQ. Rodent Treatment in Massey Hill

Do I have roof rats or Norway rats in Massey Hill?

It depends on where activity is occurring and your block's canopy coverage. Overhead scratching at night strongly suggests roof rats. Ground-level burrows or crawl space evidence suggests Norway rats. Many Massey Hill properties have both, our inspection assesses both zones before any treatment is proposed.

Why does my neighbor keep getting rats while I don't?

Rodent pressure in Massey Hill is often property-specific. Unsecured crawl space vents, gaps under garage doors, and overhanging tree branches make some properties more accessible than others. If a neighbor has persistent issues, your own perimeter inspection is worth doing regardless of current signs.
