Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Massey Hill

NC State advises screening attic and crawl space vents with quarter-inch hardware cloth and says expanding foam alone will not hold, because rats and mice chew through it. For Massey Hill homes that means metal and mesh at each opening.

NC State says gaps of about a quarter inch let mice in and a half inch lets adult rats in. At a property in Massey Hill, the inspection covers the garage and exterior door clearance, pipe and wire entries through walls and the crawl space and attic vents.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Massey Hill

  • Garage and storage: keep boxes off the floor and away from walls so droppings and nests are easy to spot.
  • Attic and crawl space vents: screen them with quarter-inch hardware cloth. Foam alone and chicken wire do not hold.
  • Rooflines and trees: UC IPM lists trimming limbs back from the roof as a way to cut the route roof rats use.
  • Trash and pet food: keep both in sealed containers off the ground, since food outside keeps a rat population close to the house.
  • Door bottoms and garage door seals: NC State advises keeping the clearance at the floor to a quarter inch or less, with kick plates or rodent-proof sweeps where doors take wear.
