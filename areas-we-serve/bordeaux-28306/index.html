FAQ. Rodent Treatment in Bordeaux

Are there more rats in Bordeaux because of the agricultural land nearby?

Yes. Agricultural land and drainage ditches adjacent to residential areas sustain higher-density Norway rat populations than purely urban environments. The rural-suburban boundary in 28306 means outdoor rodent population pressure is higher than in central Fayetteville.

My crawl space vent screens were replaced recently but I still have rodents. How?

Standard-depth vent screen installation in Sandhills sandy soil is vulnerable to Norway rat undercutting, the rat excavates below the screen edge and the screen appears intact from outside. Deep-set installation with soil compaction back against the frame is the correct fix.
