How Coats's Environment Shapes Rodent Activity

Coats sits in southern Harnett County amid significant agricultural activity. Tobacco, corn, and hog operations in the surrounding area maintain high Norway rat populations that migrate toward residential properties seasonally, especially after harvest when field food sources decline. The sandy Sandhills soil found throughout this part of Harnett County makes burrow-based entry to crawl spaces and foundations exceptionally straightforward for established rat colonies.
