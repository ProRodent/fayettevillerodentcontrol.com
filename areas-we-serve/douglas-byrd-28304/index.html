Douglas Byrd Rodent FAQ

I am near the Gray's Creek boundary. Am I getting rural field mice?

Possibly. Properties near the 28304/28306 boundary can see both house mice from suburban pressure and field mice from agricultural adjacency. Species identification at inspection confirms which you have and determines appropriate treatment.

Does commercial adjacency along Raeford Road affect my residential property?

Yes if you're within a block or two. Dumpster areas and parking lot drainage from commercial properties sustain Norway rat populations that move into adjacent residential areas. Exclusion sealing reduces access to your structure even when the outdoor source population persists.

Are crawl space inspections necessary every year for Douglas Byrd properties?

Annual inspection isn't required, but a once-every-3-years crawl space check is appropriate for properties without active infestation history. Properties that have had previous rat activity should be checked annually.

What does Norway rat burrow treatment involve?

Bait station placement at active burrow entrances, perimeter exclusion sealing to prevent re-entry, and follow-up inspection two weeks later to confirm the burrow system is no longer active. The bait stations remain in place as a long-term monitoring tool.
