Rodent Control Questions, King's Grant

How often should a Fort Liberty-area rental in Kings Grant be inspected?

For landlords with active PCS-cycle turnover, a turnover inspection between every outgoing and incoming tenant is the most effective prevention practice, particularly during the June-August peak window. Annual inspection is the minimum for properties not in the active military rental market.

Can you coordinate inspection across multiple Kings Grant rental units?

Should I remove all foundation-line landscaping to prevent rodents?

No, landscaping doesn't cause rodent problems, structural gaps do. We recommend keeping a 6-inch clearance between mulch and the foundation line, trimming back ground cover that touches the house, and never letting shrub branches contact the structure.

Are Kings Grant homes more rodent-prone than newer suburbs?

Slightly. The mature landscaping advantage that makes Kings Grant attractive aesthetically also creates more rodent harborage at the property edge. Inspection in Kings Grant pays more attention to exterior perimeter than in newer subdivisions.
