Where rodents get in

Entry Points We Check in Haymount

UC IPM notes that Norway rats dig burrows along foundations and beneath rubbish and woodpiles, while roof rats nest high in attics and wall voids. For a home in Haymount, both get checked: the ground along the foundation and the roofline.

NC State advises screening attic and crawl space vents with quarter-inch hardware cloth and says expanding foam alone will not hold, because rats and mice chew through it. For Haymount homes that means metal and mesh at each opening.

Checklist

What to Check on a Property in Haymount

  • Kitchen and pantry: store food in sealed containers. NC State puts a mouse at 50 to 75 droppings a day, so droppings in a cabinet add up fast.
  • Garage and storage: keep boxes off the floor and away from walls so droppings and nests are easy to spot.
  • Attic and crawl space vents: screen them with quarter-inch hardware cloth. Foam alone and chicken wire do not hold.
  • Rooflines and trees: UC IPM lists trimming limbs back from the roof as a way to cut the route roof rats use.
  • Trash and pet food: keep both in sealed containers off the ground, since food outside keeps a rat population close to the house.
