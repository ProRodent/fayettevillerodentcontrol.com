Fort Liberty's Rodent Control Needs

FAQ

FAQ. Rodent Treatment in Fort Liberty

What's a PCS turnover inspection?

A written rodent inspection completed during the vacancy window between an outgoing and incoming tenant. We inspect crawl space, attic, utility areas, and interior, document all findings, and provide a report confirming clean or initiating same-visit treatment.

Do you service on-post military housing?

On-post housing is managed through installation housing contracts. We serve the large volume of off-post military-affiliated properties in the 28307 ZIP and surrounding communities including Spring Lake, Kings Grant, and Long Hill.

Can you set up a recurring inspection program for my rental portfolio?

Yes. For landlords managing five or more units in the Fort Liberty corridor, we build recurring programs timed to anticipated PCS cycle windows. Call (844) 635-0403 to discuss.

Can you inspect a Fort Liberty rental between PCS tenants?

Yes. Inter-tenant inspection is one of our most common Fort Liberty work types. We can schedule between move-out and move-in, document findings, and complete any needed treatment before the new family takes possession.

What's special about treating military housing?

The turnover speed. Military rentals can change occupants every 1-3 years, which means structural conditions degrade faster than in owner-occupied homes, and exclusion work needs to be more thorough to last across multiple tenant cycles.
