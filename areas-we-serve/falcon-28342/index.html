What Drives Rodent Activity in Falcon

Falcon is a rural community in the eastern Sandhills of Cumberland County, characterized by farmland, scattered residential lots, and older structures with crawl-space foundations. The loose, sandy soil profile typical of this part of the county makes Norway rat burrowing especially easy, rats can undermine crawl space vent screens and penetrate foundation perimeters with minimal resistance.

A homeowner on a rural property near Eastover called us after harvest season, the soybean field behind the house had brought a wave of Norway rats toward the foundation. Three weeks later the homeowner reported zero new evidence, the rats that survived treatment had no entry path back into the structure.
