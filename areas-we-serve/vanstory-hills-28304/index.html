FAQ

Your Vanstory Hills Rodent Questions

What kind of rats are most common in Vanstory Hills?

Both Norway rats and roof rats are present depending on location. Norway rats dominate near commercial corridors and drainage channels. Roof rats are more active in blocks with taller established canopy overhanging rooflines. Inspection confirms which is present before treatment is proposed.

My crawl space vent screen looks intact but I still have rodents. How?

Norway rats in Sandhills sandy soil can undercut vent screens by excavating below the screen edge, the screen appears intact from outside but the gap beneath it's open. This is one of the most common entry mechanisms we find in 28304 crawl space inspections.

How often should homes in Vanstory Hills replace garage door bottom seals?

Every 2-4 years for active use, longer for low-traffic doors. The vinyl or rubber sweep at the bottom of the garage door is the single most common rodent entry point in homes of this era, and it's a homeowner-doable replacement.

Is rodent insurance worth it for older homes here?

We don't sell insurance. We offer warrantied exclusion work that includes a return-visit guarantee for a defined period, about 6-12 months depending on scope. That's the closest equivalent to insurance for completed work.
