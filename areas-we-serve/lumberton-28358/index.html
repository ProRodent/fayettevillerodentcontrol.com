Lumberton and Its Rodent Challenges

Lumberton sits on the Lumber River in central Robeson County, approximately 35-40 minutes southwest of Fayetteville. The Lumber River corridor generates significant Norway rat pressure: riverbank burrow colonies maintain continuous population, and the river's flood history, including major inundation events in recent years, periodically displaces established colonies into the residential and commercial fabric of the city.
