About Us

Locally Owned. Call-Only. Sandhills Based.

Fayetteville Rodent Control is a Cumberland County rodent removal company founded in 2023. We handle Norway rats, roof rats, and house mice across Fayetteville's neighborhoods, Fort Liberty's rental market, and the adjacent Sandhills towns within a ~45-minute drive.

Our Story

Founded in the Sandhills

Fayetteville isn't a generic pest market. The humid subtropical climate keeps rodents breeding nearly year-round. The Sandhills' loose, sandy soils make Norway rat burrowing easier than anywhere in the NC piedmont. Haymount's mature longleaf pine canopies give roof rats a highway straight to attic eave gaps. Fort Liberty's PCS-cycle rental turnovers create sustained demand that never really peaks or troughs. It just runs.

We started Fayetteville Rodent Control in 2023 specifically because the local market deserved a company that understood those dynamics without relying on a generic playbook from somewhere else. No contact forms, no automated scheduling, no call-center runaround. You call (844) 635-0403, talk to someone who can read the situation, and get a technician dispatched the same day across most of the service area.

We operate within an honest ~45-minute radius of Fayetteville. That covers all of Cumberland County, most of Harnett, Hoke, Robeson, Sampson, Bladen, and Moore County towns that fall within that drive time. We don't pad the service area with cities we can't reach in a reasonable window.
