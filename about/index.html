What this is

A Call-Only Rodent Control Service

Fayetteville Rodent Control handles Norway rats, roof rats and house mice. Every job starts with a phone call to (844) 635-0403. There are no online booking forms and no chatbots, so you describe the signs to a person and set up an inspection.

The service area is Fayetteville, the rest of Cumberland County and nearby towns within roughly a 45 minute drive. That radius is a limit, not a goal, and availability for a specific address is confirmed on the call.

How we work

Inspect, Remove, Seal, Clean

The order is always the same. An inspection identifies the species and finds the entry points. Traps remove the animals, and indoors the work does not rely on poison. Openings are closed with metal, mesh and concrete. Cleanup follows the CDC steps for wetting droppings, double bagging waste and disinfecting surfaces.

The quote is written after the inspection and depends on property size, species, the number of entry points and the amount of cleanup. This site does not list prices.
