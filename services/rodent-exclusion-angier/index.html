Why Angier Exclusion Requires Two-Pattern Approach

Angier sits in western Harnett County on the southern edge of the Raleigh-Triangle commuter zone, rural in character but growing rapidly as Triangle workers seek lower-cost housing within commuting distance of Raleigh. The result is a town with two distinct residential profiles overlapping in space. Historic Angier and the surrounding rural areas have older single-family construction (farmhouses, small-town frame homes, mid-20th-century crawl-space residences). The newer subdivisions on the outskirts have post-2005 construction with completely different entry-point patterns.

What Newer Subdivision Buyers Should Know

Buyers of newer Angier subdivision homes sometimes assume that modern construction is rodent-proof. It isn't.
