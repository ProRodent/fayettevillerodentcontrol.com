Signs Confirming Same-Day Is Right

When to Call for Same-Day Rat Removal

You've Seen a Rat

A confirmed visual sighting: in your kitchen, garage, yard, or attic. Any rat sighting means a population is already established. Don't wait.

Large Burrows at Your Foundation

2–3 inch diameter holes with smooth worn edges along your foundation, fence line, or drainage easement: Norway rat burrows in Sandhills sandy soil.

Rat-Sized Droppings Identified

Droppings 3/4 inch or longer, blunt-ended (Norway) or spindle-shaped (roof rat): confirmed rat, not mouse. Photograph and call immediately.

Heavy Overnight Attic Noise

Loud rolling, thumping, or running in the attic, distinctly heavier than house mouse movement. Roof rats in Haymount and older neighborhoods.

Gnaw Marks on Structural Materials

Large gnaw marks on wood framing, PVC pipe, or electrical conduit, rat incisor marks are substantially larger than mouse gnaw damage.

Multiple Failed Trap Attempts

Two or more weeks of store-bought trap use without the rat population declining. Population is larger than consumer-grade trapping addresses.
