🔧 Technician Insight

Residential rodent problems in Fayetteville fall into three predictable patterns by neighborhood age: pre-1960 homes (canopy access, lath-and-plaster voids), 1960-1990 (soffit gaps, slab penetrations), post-2000 (utility chase routes, attached-garage entry). The pattern tells us where to look first.

Historic-district homes (Haymount, Massey Hill, Downtown edges): Early-1900s housing with settled foundations, mature longleaf pine canopies providing roof rat travel corridors, and older attic vent configurations that haven't been updated. Roof rat pressure is highest here. Exclusion work requires heritage-compatible materials that don't damage original wood trim, masonry, or roofline.

Mid-century and 1970s–80s builds (Bonnie Doone, Douglas Byrd, Terry Sanford): Slab and crawl space construction with mixed pest history: Norway rats at foundation level, house mice through utility gaps, and aging crawl space vapor barriers that create moisture-driven harborage.

Newer suburban builds (Cliffdale, Arran Lakes, Pine Forest, Kings Grant): Sandhills sandy-soil crawl space concerns, landscaping that provides ground-level cover near foundation gaps, and newer but less settled utility penetrations. FAQ

Residential Rodent Control Questions

Does the age of my Fayetteville home affect rodent treatment?

Yes, significantly. Older Haymount and Massey Hill homes from the early 1900s have settled foundations, deteriorated original seals, and attic structures that roof rats access via tree canopy. Newer Cliffdale and Arran Lakes builds have Sandhills sandy-soil crawl space concerns and newer construction gaps. The inspection approach and exclusion materials differ by housing era.

Are your treatments safe for children and pets?

Yes. Interior living areas use snap traps rather than rodenticide. Exterior bait stations are tamper-resistant and placed where children and pets can't access them. Placement is discussed before any work begins, and we'll advise on precautions specific to your household during the call.

Do you offer a warranty on residential exclusion work?

Yes. Exclusion sealing: hardware cloth, caulk, expanding foam, vent screen replacement, and structural gap filling, carries a warranty against re-entry through treated entry points. Warranty terms are confirmed in writing at the time of service.

How long does residential rodent treatment take from first call to clearance?

A light house mouse infestation in a well-maintained home can clear in 2–3 weeks from first visit. Moderate rat infestations often require 3–5 weeks including follow-up visits before exclusion sealing is appropriate. Larger properties or heavy infestations take longer. Timeline is estimated after the initial inspection and included in the written plan.
