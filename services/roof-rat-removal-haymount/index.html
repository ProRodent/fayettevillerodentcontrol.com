Why Haymount Has Fayetteville's Worst Roof Rat Pressure

Roof rats (Rattus rattus) are arboreal rather than ground-dwelling, traveling along tree branches, power lines, and other elevated pathways to reach attic-level entry points. Haymount's defining characteristic, the dense mature longleaf pine canopy that runs the length of the historic district, is exactly the kind of arboreal pathway that lets roof rats reach houses that ground-level Norway rats can't access.

🔧 Technician Insight

Roof rat infestations in Haymount and other canopy-heavy neighborhoods almost always have multiple entry points, not one. Sealing the obvious one without finding the others guarantees re-entry within weeks. Our thermal imaging shows every active travel route, not just the visible signs.

The pairing of dense canopy with early-1900s architecture creates the ideal roof rat situation. Haymount housing dates predominantly to the 1900s-1930s, with eave-to-soffit junctions, gable vents, and ridge details that have settled and shifted over 90-120 years. The gaps that develop at those junctions are pencil-width or larger, well within roof rat entry capability. Treatment here requires inspecting at attic level, not just ground level.

We approach Haymount roof rat work with the canopy as part of the treatment plan. Canopy trimming (maintaining 4-6 feet clearance between branches and the roofline), attic-level entry sealing, and trapping inside the attic to address the established population work together. Call (844) 635-0403.

FAQ

Haymount Roof Rat FAQ

How can I tell if I have roof rats vs Norway rats in Haymount?

Sounds are the easiest indicator. Roof rats produce overhead activity sounds: scratching, scurrying in the attic or ceiling, particularly at dusk and dawn. Norway rats produce ground-level sounds and basement/crawl-space activity. In Haymount specifically, attic activity is almost always roof rats.

Do I really need to trim my mature pine trees?

Selective trimming, yes, maintain 4-6 feet of clearance between branches and the roofline. Full tree removal isn't necessary and is rarely the right solution. The trimming eliminates the rodent access pathway without sacrificing canopy character.

Will roof rats damage my historic attic insulation and framing?

Yes if untreated. Roof rats nest in attic insulation, gnaw on wood and electrical wiring, and contaminate the space with droppings and urine. Damage compounds over months. Treatment timing matters.

How long does Haymount roof rat treatment take?

Initial inspection and trapping setup: one visit, 2-3 hours. Active trapping and removal: 2-3 weeks of weekly checks. Exclusion sealing and canopy trimming coordination: separate visit, usually same week as trapping conclusion.

What does Haymount roof rat treatment typically cost?

Combined inspection, trapping, exclusion, and canopy trimming coordination most often ranges from $1,200 to $2,800 depending on infestation extent and exclusion scope. We provide a written quote before work starts.
