Guest Notification and Timing Protocol

When a guest reports a rodent sighting mid-stay, the critical window is 24–48 hours. After that, most guests will document the issue in their review regardless of resolution. Our same-day dispatch is designed around this timeline. For turnover treatments between guests, we have more latitude: exterior bait stations, crawl space treatment, attic inspection, and full exclusion sealing can all be completed during the vacancy window.
