Lillington Residential Property Profiles

Lillington's residential housing falls into two broad profiles. The older homes concentrated around the historic courthouse square date predominantly to the early-1900s through the mid-1900s. The period when Lillington consolidated its role as Harnett County seat. These properties share the construction patterns of NC small-town county-seat housing: brick or block foundations with crawl space or partial-basement construction, deteriorated mortar joints, original galvanized vent infrastructure, and cumulative entry-point profiles typical of long-standing homes.

🔧 Technician Insight

Residential rodent problems in Fayetteville fall into three predictable patterns by neighborhood age: pre-1960 homes (canopy access, lath-and-plaster voids), 1960-1990 (soffit gaps, slab penetrations), post-2000 (utility chase routes, attached-garage entry). The pattern tells us where to look first.

The newer suburban development that has grown around Lillington over the past 30 years has substantially different construction patterns: post-1990 frame construction, slab-on-grade or full-basement foundations, modern utility-penetration sealing, and the entry-point patterns specific to newer suburban building (garage seals, soffit gaps, slab utility penetrations).
