FAQ

Hope Mills Rodent Exclusion FAQ

Why does exclusion approach matter for Hope Mills more than other Cumberland County areas?

Because Hope Mills' housing diversity spans 50+ years of construction practices. A treatment template that works for one era doesn't fit the next. Proper exclusion here requires more upfront assessment work than in single-vintage neighborhoods.

How long does properly installed Hope Mills exclusion last?

5-7 years on average before reinspection is recommended. Properties with high garage traffic see garage seal wear faster (2-4 years for that component); foundation-level work generally lasts longer.

Will exclusion work be visible from inside or outside the home?

Most exterior work is minimally visible, copper mesh and silicone at gaps blend with foundation and siding colors. Interior work is invisible after completion. Vent screen replacement may be noticeable but uses period-appropriate materials.

Can I do exclusion work myself instead of hiring it out?

Some yes (garage door bottom seal replacement is straightforward DIY). Most foundation-level work, vent screen installation, and utility-penetration sealing requires specific tools and material knowledge that homeowners typically don't have.

Does exclusion work come with a warranty?

Yes, usually a 6-12 month return-visit warranty depending on scope. Heavier-scope work carries longer warranty; targeted single-point work carries shorter coverage.
