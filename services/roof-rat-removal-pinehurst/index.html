Pinehurst's character, mature longleaf pine canopy throughout the village, golf-course landscape corridors connecting residential properties to course-edge habitat, and the cottage-era architecture with hand-cut soffit and gable details, combines to make Pinehurst one of the more persistent roof rat environments in our service area. 🔧 Technician Insight

Roof rat infestations in Haymount and other canopy-heavy neighborhoods almost always have multiple entry points, not one. Sealing the obvious one without finding the others guarantees re-entry within weeks. Our thermal imaging shows every active travel route, not just the visible signs.

Resort-era cottage architecture (1895 through the 1930s for most original village structures) has eave-to-soffit junctions, ridge vent details, and gable louver patterns that have settled and shifted over a century. The gaps that develop at those junctions are pencil-width or larger and are well within roof rat entry capability. Treatment here requires both ground-level intervention (canopy access management, perimeter inspection) and attic-level work (trapping inside the established harborage, sealing entry points from the inside).

Treatment Process

How We Handle This Service in Pinehurst

1

Canopy & Roof Inspection

Roof rats access via the longleaf pine canopy in Fayetteville. We inspect roof line, soffit gaps, vent stack flashing, gable vents, and overhanging branches.

2

Interior Trapping

Snap traps in attic travel routes. Roof rats are neophobic; placement is based on grease marks and droppings, not guesswork.

3

Roof & Soffit Sealing

Hardware cloth at vent openings, sheet-metal flashing at soffit-fascia junctions, structural sealant on roof penetrations. Tree-trimming recommendations made where canopy access can be eliminated.

4

Attic Sanitization

EPA-registered disinfectant on droppings, urine trails, and nesting material. Damaged insulation removal and replacement quoted separately if needed.
