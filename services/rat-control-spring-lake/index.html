Why Spring Lake Rat Treatment Is Different From Fayetteville

Spring Lake's economy and housing stock are shaped by Fort Liberty's proximity, rental properties dominate the residential mix, and military families cycle through every 1-3 years on PCS rotation. This turnover dynamic affects rat treatment in two specific ways. First, structural conditions degrade faster than in owner-occupied housing (more wear, deferred maintenance accumulates differently). Second, the brief vacancy windows between tenants are when populations establish or expand without notice.

🔧 Technician Insight

The most common mistake after spotting rat droppings: homeowners set snap traps and assume the problem is solved when traps stop catching. But trap success drops to near zero once colony members learn to avoid the location, they don't leave, they just route around. The actual measure of success is sealing entry points, not body count.

Spring Lake sits along the Lower Little River drainage, and properties closer to the river or its tributaries see elevated Norway rat pressure from the corridor habitat. The town's older central blocks have housing dating to the 1940s-60s with foundation-condition issues typical of that era; the newer subdivisions on the perimeter have post-2000 construction with different entry-point patterns.
