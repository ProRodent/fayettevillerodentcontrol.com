Why Southern Pines Exclusion Is Property-Specific

Southern Pines housing diversity makes generic exclusion templates inadequate here. Resort-era homes built between the 1890s and 1930s near downtown have foundation-level conditions characteristic of that era: brick with deteriorated mortar, corroded vent screens, original utility-penetration sealing that hasn't been touched in decades. Post-2000 subdivisions on the area's perimeter have slab-on-grade construction with different entry-point patterns: garage seal failure, soffit gap development, utility penetrations.

Equestrian Property Treatment Approach

Southern Pines equestrian properties present a treatment challenge that pure residential work doesn't. Feed and hay storage attracts rats and mice in volumes that residential properties don't see; barn and stable construction allows entry through gaps that no residential code would permit; the property layout typically puts residential structures within rodent travel range of the outbuildings. Treatment scope for equestrian properties has to include all structures, exterior bait stations at appropriate exterior locations, ongoing monitoring rather than one-time work, and integration with the property's existing feed-storage and barn-management practices. We coordinate with the property owner on barn-side modifications that complement the residential treatment.

Most Southern Pines exclusion work pairs with perimeter bait station programs for equestrian properties and rat control treatment if active populations are confirmed.
