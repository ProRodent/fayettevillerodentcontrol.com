FAQ

Emergency Service Questions

What counts as a rodent emergency?

Any situation where active rodent presence creates immediate health, safety, or property risk: a rat or mouse confirmed in a living space or kitchen; a child's room with confirmed activity; overnight attic intrusion with loud persistent noise; dead rodent with active odor in an inaccessible wall or duct; or a commercial food-service property with active infestation ahead of a health inspection.

How quickly can you respond to a rodent emergency in Fayetteville?

We dispatch same-day across Cumberland County. For calls received before 8 PM, same-day arrival is standard for most Fayetteville addresses. Overnight calls are queued for first-light dispatch unless the situation requires overnight response, which is available for genuine emergencies.

Is emergency service more expensive?

We provide an upfront quote by phone before dispatch. Emergency pricing reflects same-day availability but is quoted transparently before any work begins. We don't bill surprise surcharges on arrival.

What should I do until the technician arrives?

For a live rodent in a living space: keep children and pets away from the area and close interior doors to limit movement. Don't attempt to corner or capture it, startled rats bite. For dead rodent odor: improve ventilation and avoid disturbing walls or ceilings. For attic noise: leave attic-access areas undisturbed. We'll advise further on the call.
