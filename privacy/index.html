Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Fayetteville Rodent Control ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you visit fayettevillerodentcontrol.com or contact us by phone.

Information We Collect

Because we operate as a call-only business with no contact forms, no account creation, and no online booking, we collect very limited personal information directly from site visitors. Information we may collect includes:

  • Phone numbers and call details when you call (844) 635-0403 to request service
  • Service address and property information provided during scheduling calls
  • Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, referral source) collected automatically when you visit our website
  • Email address if you contact us via the email address listed on this site

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule and dispatch rodent control services to your location
  • To contact you regarding your service appointment, follow-up inspections, or warranty work
  • To improve our website and understand which content is most useful to visitors
  • To comply with North Carolina pest control licensing recordkeeping requirements

We don't

  • Sell, rent, or share your personal information with third-party marketers
  • Use your information to send unsolicited marketing communications
  • Use session recording tools, heatmap software, or behavioral tracking beyond standard analytics
  • Collect sensitive personal information such as financial data, government IDs, or health information

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services including Google Fonts (for typography) and standard web analytics. These services may collect limited technical information (such as IP address and browser type) in accordance with their own privacy policies. We don't control these third parties' data practices.

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies required for basic site functionality. We don't use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though this may affect certain site features.

Data Retention

We retain service records for as long as required by North Carolina pest control licensing regulations and for reasonable warranty recordkeeping. Web server logs are retained for standard security and diagnostic purposes.

Your Rights

You may contact us at contact@fayettevillerodentcontrol.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, where applicable under law.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Reach us at contact@fayettevillerodentcontrol.com or by phone at (844) 635-0403.

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be noted with an updated date above.
